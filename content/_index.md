title: European Coaching Program
sections:
  - type: introduction
    strapline: Developing barbershop coaches across Europe
    summary: >-
      The European Coaching Program (ECP) will train and certify barbershop
      coaches all over Europe. Our aim is to develop experienced coaches into
      world-class coaches. This will further strengthen barbershop expertise in
      Europe and make it easier for quartets and choruses to access high-level
      coaching.
    period: The program runs from the spring of 2027 to the autumn of 2029.
    dates:
      - date: End of September 2026
        event: Selection team announced
      - date: 1–15 November 2026
        event: Applications open
      - date: 15 December 2026
        event: Candidates selected

  - type: program
    heading: Program
    subheading: Two years of training and practice
    introduction: >-
      In the ECP, coaches with some prior experience will deepen their mastery
      of coaching techniques. Candidates learn to assess the strengths and
      growth opportunities of an ensemble, give positive feedback, set
      priorities, choose impactful interventions, communicate clearly, run an
      effective coaching session, and conduct themselves as coaches.
    lead_in: "The two-year training program consists of the following elements:"
    components:
      - heading: European Harmony Academy
        text: Candidates attend the ECP stream in August 2027 and August 2028.
        link_label: European Harmony Academy
        link_url: https://www.barbershop.de/en/veranstaltungen/eha
      - heading: Coaching practice
        text: At least five ensemble coaching sessions per year, with feedback from the ensembles.
      - heading: Apprenticeship
        text: At least one Harmony College or comparable event as a coaching apprentice.
      - heading: Mentoring
        text: Several calls per year with an experienced international coach or judge, based partly on coaching videos.
      - heading: Peer review
        text: Quarterly review of coaching recordings with another candidate.
      - heading: Online classes
        text: Classes every few months on coaching practice, ethics and the BHS scoring categories.

  - type: applications
    heading: Applications
    subheading: Who should apply
    body: |-
      The ECP is intended for people who already coach quartets or choruses and have a solid knowledge of the barbershop style. Applicants should be open to feedback and able to take part throughout 2027 and 2028.

      The application will include coaching videos and written questions about coaching experience, approach and motivation. Shortlisted applicants will be interviewed online.

      Applications are assessed by an international selection team. The main criteria are current coaching ability, barbershop knowledge and willingness to learn.
    cost_label: Expected personal cost
    cost: Approximately €3,000 over two years, mainly for travel and attendance at two European Harmony Academies. Candidates may seek support from their national society.

  - type: certification
    heading: Certification
    body: |-
      Candidates have a mid-term review after the first year. This results in an individual development plan for the second year.

      Final interviews take place in late 2028. Certification is based on coaching videos, feedback from ensembles and the candidate’s development during the program.

      Coaching videos and application material will be handled under a GDPR-compliant data management plan published before applications open.

  - type: background
    heading: Background
    subheading: The BinG! Coaching Certification Program
    body: |-
      The ECP is based on the Coaching Certification Program run by Barbershop in Germany from 2019 to 2023. The CCP certified six coaches. The ECP adapts that program for candidates from across Europe.

      Four CCP-certified coaches later qualified as BHS judges.
