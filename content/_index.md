# European Coaching Program

*Developing barbershop coaches across Europe*

The European Coaching Program (ECP) will train and certify barbershop coaches all over Europe.
Our aim is to develop experienced coaches into world-class coaches.
This will further strengthen barbershop expertise in Europe and make it easier for
quartets and choruses to access high-level coaching.

The program runs from the spring of 2027 to the autumn of 2029.

## Program

### Two years of training and practice

In the ECP, coaches with some prior experience will deepen their mastery of coaching techniques.
Candidates learn to assess the strengths and growth opportunities of an ensemble,
give positive feedback,
set priorities,
choose impactful interventions,
communicate clearly,
run an effective coaching session,
and conduct themselves as coaches.

The two-year training program consists of the following elements:

#### European Harmony Academy

Candidates attend the ECP stream at the [European Harmony Academy](https://www.barbershop.de/en/veranstaltungen/eha) in August 2027 and August 2028.

#### Coaching practice

At least five ensemble coaching sessions per year, with feedback from the ensembles.

#### Apprenticeship

At least one Harmony College or comparable event as a coaching apprentice.

#### Mentoring

Several calls per year with an experienced international coach or judge, based partly on coaching videos.

#### Peer review

Quarterly review of coaching recordings with another candidate.

#### Online classes

Classes every few months on coaching practice, ethics and the BHS scoring categories.

## Applications

### Who should apply

The ECP is intended for people who already coach quartets or choruses and have a solid knowledge of the barbershop style. Applicants should be open to feedback and able to take part throughout 2027 and 2028.

The application will include coaching videos and written questions about coaching experience, approach and motivation. Shortlisted applicants will be interviewed online.

Applications are assessed by an international selection team. The main criteria are current coaching ability, barbershop knowledge and willingness to learn.

**Expected personal cost:** Approximately €3,000 over two years, mainly for travel and attendance at two European Harmony Academies. Candidates may seek support from their national society.

## Certification

Candidates have a mid-term review after the first year. This results in an individual development plan for the second year.

Final interviews take place in late 2028. Certification is based on coaching videos, feedback from ensembles and the candidate’s development during the program.

Coaching videos and application material will be handled under a GDPR-compliant data management plan published before applications open.

## Background

### The BinG! Coaching Certification Program

The ECP is based on the Coaching Certification Program run by Barbershop in Germany from 2019 to 2023. The CCP certified six coaches. The ECP adapts that program for candidates from across Europe.

Four CCP-certified coaches later qualified as BHS judges.
